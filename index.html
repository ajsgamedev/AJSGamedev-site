Professional work

Seven years of client work in VR and AR training.

VR training & step-by-step 3D guides

Craftguide · 2019–2026

I turned paper manuals into interactive 3D training. Using models and lit scenes from the 3D team, I built the step-by-step logic. At each step a voiceover plays, and an icon and highlight show which part to work on: removing a cover, fitting an air filter, routing a cable.

Each guide shipped as a VR experience and as a tap-through mobile version. 30+ apps in total, each with 1–10 training modules, for clients including Stiebel Eltron and Pluggit.

I also added features to the in-house VR framework: teleport movement, joystick turning and voiceover repeat. I built them in Blueprint so they were quick to develop and deploy.

Unreal Engine · Blueprint · OpenXR · PICO · Meta Quest · HTC Vive

Press: Stiebel Eltron Press: Pluggit

HoloLens AR apps

Holo-Light · 2018–2019

Augmented reality apps for Microsoft HoloLens: step-by-step task guidance, product demos, and interactive model customisation.

Highlight: the Cabin Configurator, a HoloLens app I built for Fritzmeier Cabs that was shown at bauma 2019. Visitors scanned a marker on a real cabin and a 3D model appeared overlaid on it. They could then change its colour and swap the lights and interior options.

Unity · C# · MRTK · Vuforia · HoloLens
